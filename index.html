Tube Runner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026
Developer / contact: Idan Leizerovich – tuberunnerx@gmail.com

This policy explains what data the Android game "Tube Runner" ("the game") collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

1. Data stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is not sent to our servers:

  • Optional profile details you enter on the Profile screen: email address and profile photo.
  • Images you choose for the "My Own Image" skin. Face detection and background removal for this feature run on your device; the image itself is not uploaded.
  • Game progress: coins, owned and selected skins, and audio preferences.

To run the face-detection feature, the game downloads open model files and code libraries (Google MediaPipe) from public servers (cdn.jsdelivr.net, unpkg.com, storage.googleapis.com). These servers may receive your IP address as part of a normal download request.

2. Data sent to our servers (Google Firebase)

If you set a username and achieve a high score, the following is sent to Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to power the public leaderboard:

  • Your username (up to 20 characters),
  • Your high score and the time it was saved,
  • An anonymous Firebase user ID (no sign-in or email is required).

Your username and score are shown publicly to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or personal information as a username.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are shown a consent form before personalized ads are used. See how Google uses data in ads. You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Purchases (Google Play)

In-game purchases (coin packs and the custom image skin) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. All purchases are final and we do not offer refunds ourselves; refund requests are handled by Google under its own policy. Nothing here limits any rights you may have under applicable law.

5. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play, AdMob)
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

6. Deleting your data

  • In the app: open Terms & Privacy Policy and tap Delete All My Data. This erases the data stored on your device and removes your leaderboard entry from our servers. Profile details (name, email, photo) can also be removed from the My Profile screen.
  • Without the app: if you have uninstalled the game or cannot access it, email tuberunnerx@gmail.com with your username and we will delete your leaderboard entry. Note that the anonymous ID cannot be linked to you without the username you entered.
  • Uninstalling the game removes locally stored data, except possibly data restored through Android/Google backup.

Data collected by Google (for example advertising data) is managed through your Google account settings.

7. Data retention

Leaderboard entries are kept until you delete them or we remove them. Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted to Firebase over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact
